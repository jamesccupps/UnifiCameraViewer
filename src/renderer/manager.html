    Add console

    Sign in with a local-only UniFi OS account (not your UI.com login). Protect "View Only" access is enough.

    Save layout

    Saves every camera window on screen: where it is, its size, quality, 360° views and options. Saving under an existing name replaces that layout.

    New layout

      Drag a camera onto a tile, or double-click it to fill the next empty one. Drag a tile onto another to swap them.
