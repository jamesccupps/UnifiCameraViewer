UniFi Protect live view for Windows, one window per camera, across several consoles. Project page: https://jamesccupps.github.io/UnifiCameraViewer/

A fix for camera PCs that get slower the longer the app runs.

### Fixed
- **Each camera's stream now starts over every 30 minutes or so.** A camera window's CPU use kept growing the longer its stream ran. On a 14-window PC it was about 4 % of a core after a restart, plus 9 % for every hour after that. Within a few hours the PC was flat out, streams fell behind and kept restarting, and other programs beside it (exacqVision) struggled.
  - Now each stream starts over 30 to 37.5 minutes after it last started. The extra time varies at random, so windows don't all do it at once.
  - The picture holds its last frame for the moment it takes, the same as Reconnect stream. There's no "Reconnecting…" and no black.
  - The hourly line in `protect-panes.log` now ends with `timed refreshes`, how many there were that hour. Restarts after a problem are still counted on their own.

### Install or upgrade
- **From 1.4.13, 1.4.14 or 1.4.15:** Settings → Updates → **Check for updates**, then **Install**. It's a small download (a couple of MB): only the app's own file changes.
- **From 1.3.x to 1.4.12:** the same, but it downloads the full installer (about 120 MB) or zip (about 160 MB), because those copies are on an older Electron.
- **Installed with Scoop:** `scoop update protectpanes`. On a 1.4.6 copy from Scoop, use that rather than the app's **Install** button: 1.4.6 would still install this update itself, and a full update from inside the app confuses Scoop's records of it.
- **From 1.2.0 or 1.3.0 portable:** quit the app (tray → Quit) and run `ProtectPanes-Setup.exe`, or replace the folder with the zip. Your settings carry over.
- **New install:** run `ProtectPanes-Setup.exe`. It's signed by James Cupps. SmartScreen may still warn about a new download until it has seen enough of them: click **More info**, check the publisher, then **Run anyway**.

### Files
- `ProtectPanes-Setup.exe`: the installer.
- `ProtectPanes-win32-x64.zip`: the portable app (also what Scoop installs).
- `ProtectPanes-app.asar.gz`: the small update, for copies already on Electron 45.4.1 (1.4.13 to 1.4.15).
- `latest.json`: the signed manifest the in-app updater reads.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`: the SHA-256 of each file above, for checking a download by hand.
